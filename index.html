Kidney Stone Prevention Guide

1. Why Fluid Volume is #1 Defense ▼

Kidney stones form when minerals in urine become supersaturated and crystallize. High fluid intake directly dilutes calcium, oxalate, and uric acid, preventing nucleation. Studies show maintaining 2.5+ L of urine output slashes stone recurrence by over 50%.

2. The Power of Dietary Citrate ▼

Citrate is nature's potent crystal inhibitor. It binds with urinary calcium, preventing it from binding with oxalate. Squeeze fresh lemon or lime juice into water to naturally raise urinary citrate and pH.

3. Dietary Oxalate & Calcium Pairing ▼

Do not avoid dietary calcium! When you consume calcium alongside oxalate foods (spinach, beets, nuts, chocolate), they bind in the gut and exit harmlessly in stool rather than being absorbed into the kidneys.

4. Sodium & Animal Protein Moderation ▼

Excess dietary salt forces the kidneys to excrete more calcium into urine (hypercalciuria). High animal protein increases uric acid and lowers urine pH, promoting both calcium oxalate and uric acid stones.

5. The Critical Overnight Window ▼

Urine is most concentrated and acidic between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM. Always drink a glass of water right before sleep and upon waking to protect the kidneys during the night.

🛡️ Clinical Medical Disclaimer
HydroStone is an educational hydration companion for kidney stone patients. It is not a medical device and does not provide formal diagnosis or treatment plans. Patients with chronic kidney disease (CKD), congestive heart failure, or prescribed fluid limitations must consult their urologist or nephrologist before altering daily fluid intake.

Gentle Stone-Risk Tip

Dark colas frequently contain phosphoric acid, and strong black teas are rich in soluble oxalates. Both can heighten kidney stone precipitation risk.

Best Practice: Enjoy in moderation, and always drink an extra glass of water immediately afterward to keep urinary solutes dilute!
